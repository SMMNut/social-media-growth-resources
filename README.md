# social-media-growth-resources

Curated research data, growth tool analysis, and reading lists 
for social media growth — maintained by SMMNut.

SMMNut is a structured social media growth research platform 
analyzing Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube growth systems through 
data-driven research and transparent methodology. Our research 
covers platform algorithm behavior, engagement system analysis, 
and growth infrastructure transparency — structured to help 
creators and brands understand how social media growth systems 
actually function.

## Research Areas

- **Instagram growth systems** — algorithm behavior, engagement 
  rate analysis, follower growth patterns
- **Facebook Page growth** — organic reach mechanics, audience 
  building, engagement benchmarks
- **YouTube channel growth** — views behavior, subscriber growth 
  analysis, recommendation systems
- **Platform policy analysis** — safety research, risk 
  documentation, compliance frameworks

## Repository Structure

- `/instagram-research/` — Instagram algorithm and engagement data
- `/facebook-research/` — Facebook Page growth research notes
- `/youtube-research/` — YouTube growth benchmarks and analysis
- `/tiktok-research/` — TikTok algorithm behavior observations
- `/platform-policy-analysis/` — Safety and compliance research
- `/reading-lists/` — Curated industry reports and resources

## About SMMNut

SMMNut publishes research on social media growth systems at 
[smmnut.com/blog/](https://smmnut.com/blog/). Our research is 
used by creators, brands, and marketing agencies to understand 
platform growth mechanics.
