# Growth Tools Analysis

Evaluation frameworks, tool comparisons, and curated resource lists 
for social media growth tools — maintained by SMMNut.

SMMNut evaluates growth tools across Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, 
TikTok, and X using a structured research methodology focused on 
platform compliance, delivery reliability, and account safety outcomes.

## Research Framework

Our tool evaluations assess:

- **Platform coverage** — which social media platforms the tool supports
- **Delivery methodology** — how growth is delivered and at what speed
- **Account safety profile** — compliance with platform terms of service
- **Targeting capability** — audience quality and demographic options
- **Reliability indicators** — consistency of results over time
- **Refill and retention policy** — how providers handle follower drop-off

## Data Files

- `social-media-growth-tools-comparison.md` — structured comparison 
  of growth tool categories across platforms
- `tool-evaluation-framework.md` — criteria and scoring methodology 
  used in SMMNut research assessments

## Tool Categories Covered

- Instagram growth tools — follower and engagement services
- Facebook Page growth tools — likes, reach, and engagement services
- YouTube growth tools — views, subscribers, and watch time services
- TikTok growth tools — followers, views, and engagement services
- Cross-platform growth services — multi-network providers

## Research Notes

Tool evaluations are based on publicly available information, 
platform documentation, and structured observational research. 
SMMNut does not endorse any specific third-party tool or service.
